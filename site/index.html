Why VIDA LAB exists

Built from the experience of wanting better answers.

VIDA LAB was founded by Rowan Albritton after her own experiences navigating confusing symptoms and learning how difficult it can be to understand a changing body when the information around you feels fragmented, intimidating, or incomplete.

The goal is not to replace clinicians or turn every symptom into a conclusion. It is to make credible health information more understandable, make uncertainty visible, and give people better language for the questions they want to ask.
