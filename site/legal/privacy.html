Privacy Policy

We cannot read your symptoms, your check-ins, or anything the app reads from Apple Health. Not because we promise not to — because it is encrypted on your phone, with a key we never receive, before it is stored anywhere.

Last updated 17 September 2026

This policy explains what stays on your device, what is backed up in a form we cannot open, the few things that travel in readable form, and what we do and don't do with them. It applies to the VIDA LAB iOS app and this website.

1. Health information is stored on your device

Everything you record in VIDA LAB is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • Check-ins, symptoms, severity ratings and notes
  • Your health picture, conditions and medications
  • Experiments, patterns and weekly reports
  • Your name and profile photo, if you add them
  • Anything read from Apple Health

Your device is always the original. If you never create an account, none of it is uploaded anywhere at all, and deleting the app deletes it.

2. If you sign in, your entries are backed up — encrypted

An account exists for one reason: so that a year of your own records survives a lost, stolen or broken phone. That requires a copy somewhere other than your phone. Here is exactly how that copy works.

Before anything is uploaded, your check-ins, experiments, Doctor Prep snapshots and saved articles are encrypted on your iPhone using AES-GCM with a 256-bit key generated on your device. That key is never sent to us and we never possess it. It is held in your iPhone's Keychain and, so that a new phone can read your history, in your iCloud Keychain — which Apple protects with end-to-end encryption that also excludes Apple.

What our database physically contains for each entry is a block of ciphertext, the calendar date the entry belongs to, and your account identifier. That is all. We cannot see a symptom, a severity score, a note, a medication, an experiment result or which articles you saved — not for a support request, not for a subpoena, not for our own curiosity. The date is kept readable because syncing needs to know which day an entry belongs to; it reveals that an entry exists, never what it says.

The honest cost of real encryption: if you lose your device and your iCloud Keychain, your backup cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. There is no master key and no reset link. We consider that the correct trade-off for health data, but you should know it before you rely on the backup.

Your name, profile photo, cycle settings, report address and app preferences are not uploaded at all — they stay on your device only.

3. Apple Health is read-only

If you grant permission, VIDA LAB reads the following from Apple Health to save you re-typing what your phone already knows:

  • Sleep analysis
  • Menstrual flow
  • Headache, abdominal cramps and fatigue
  • Step count and exercise minutes

Three commitments about this data:

  • We never write to Apple Health. The app requests read access only and holds no permission to add, change or delete anything in your Health app.
  • We never receive it in readable form. Health data read from Apple Health is used on-device to fill in your timeline. If it forms part of a check-in that gets backed up, it is encrypted first, exactly like everything you type by hand.
  • You can revoke it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › VIDA LAB. The app keeps working; it simply stops importing.

Anything you enter by hand always takes priority over an imported value. We never use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, or to build a profile of you, and we never share it with third parties.

4. Ask Vida answers on your phone

Ask Vida draws on a library of cited research that ships inside the app. Your questions are answered on-device and are not sent to us, to an AI provider, or to anyone else. Nothing you ask is logged off your phone.

5. What actually leaves your device

Accounts are optional

The entire app works without one, and signed out nothing is uploaded at all. If you do sign in, we store your sign-in identity — an account identifier, your email address, and your name and profile picture if your provider supplies them — and the encrypted backup described in section 2. Encrypted entries are linked to your account so that your phone can find them again; that link tells us which rows are yours, never what they contain. Your session is kept in your device's Keychain.

Reports go through your mail app

When you send a weekly report or doctor summary, VIDA LAB opens Apple's mail composer with the report attached. You choose the recipient and press send. The report travels through your own email account. It does not pass through us, and we never see it or its contents.

Subscriptions

Vida+ is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active; for that purpose RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, the product purchased, purchase and renewal dates, and basic device and country information. It receives no health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

6. What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information. Ever.
  • We do not show you ads, and we do not share data with ad networks.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use your data for advertising or data-broker purposes.
  • We do not use your health information to train machine-learning models — and could not, since we cannot read it.
  • On a Family plan, the person who pays can see the subscription. They cannot see anyone's health logs — each person's data stays on their own device.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on your device: Settings › Clear all data, inside the app. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Delete your account: Settings › Delete account. This deletes every backed-up row, removes your sign-in record, and destroys the encryption key on your device — so any copy that somehow outlived the deletion could never be opened by anyone.
  • Revoke Apple Health access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Access, correction and portability: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal information we hold, and to object to certain processing. Because the only personal information we hold is your optional sign-in identity, these requests are satisfied by deleting your account — or you can email us and we will respond within 30 days. Note that we cannot produce a readable export of your backup, because we cannot decrypt it; your own device holds the readable copy, and the app can email you a report from it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

8. Retention and security

On-device data stays until you delete it or remove the app. Encrypted backup rows are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete it. Sign-in records follow the same lifetime. Session tokens and your encryption key are stored in the iOS Keychain, all connections use encrypted HTTPS, and every row is additionally protected by database rules that allow only your own signed-in session to reach it.

No system is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection here is architectural rather than a promise: a breach of our database would expose ciphertext and dates, because the keys that open it are not there to steal.

9. Children

VIDA LAB is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Anyone under the age of majority where they live should have a parent or guardian review these terms with them before subscribing.

10. International use

We operate from the United Kingdom. If you create an account, your sign-in information and your encrypted backup may be processed in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, or the United States by our providers under appropriate safeguards. Because health entries cross borders only as ciphertext we cannot open, no provider or jurisdiction they sit in can read them.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change is material — particularly anything that would alter what leaves your device — we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, and ask for your agreement where the law requires it.

Questions, requests, or anything in this document that doesn't match what you're seeing: privacy@vidalab.co.
