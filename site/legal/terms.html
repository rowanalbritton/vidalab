1. What VIDA LAB is

VIDA LAB is a self-tracking tool for people living with chronic illness. You record how you feel, and the app shows you patterns in your own data — things worth noticing and worth raising with a clinician. It is a wellness and journalling tool, not a medical device.

2. Not medical advice

VIDA LAB does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Nothing in the app — no pattern, insight, experiment result, research citation, report, or answer from Ask Vida — is medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation. Always seek the advice of your doctor or another qualified health professional with any question about a medical condition, and never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you read in VIDA LAB.

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

About patterns and correlations

The patterns VIDA LAB surfaces describe things that appeared together in your own records. They do not establish that one thing caused another. Two things can move together by coincidence, or because of a third factor the app knows nothing about. Treat every pattern as a question to explore with a clinician, not an answer.

About the research library

Ask Vida answers from a library of published research that ships inside the app. Citations are provided so you can read the source yourself. Research is summarised for readability, may be superseded by newer findings, and describes populations rather than you specifically. We don't guarantee that any cited study applies to your situation.

About Apple Health data

Where you allow it, VIDA LAB reads data from Apple Health. The accuracy of that data depends on the devices and apps that produced it, which are outside our control. Don't rely on it for clinical decisions.

4. Your data, and the limits of recovery

VIDA LAB stores your health information on your device. If you sign in, an encrypted copy is also backed up so your records can survive a lost phone — but it is encrypted on your device first, with a key we never receive. This is a deliberate privacy decision, and it has one important consequence: we cannot read your data, and we cannot recover it for you.

There is no master key and no reset link. If you lose access to your device and your iCloud Keychain, the backup cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us. If you clear your data in Settings or delete your account, it is gone permanently. Keep your own copies — iCloud device backups, or by emailing yourself reports — if your records matter to you.

See our Privacy Policy for exactly what we hold and what we cannot see.
