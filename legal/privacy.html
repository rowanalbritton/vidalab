Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

VIDA LAB is built so that your health information never leaves your phone. We cannot read your symptoms, your check-ins, or anything the app reads from Apple Health — not because we promise not to, but because that data is never sent to us in the first place.

This policy explains exactly what stays on your device, the few things that do travel, and what we do and don't do with them. It applies to the VIDA LAB iOS app and this website.

1. Health information stays on your device

Everything you record in VIDA LAB is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • Check-ins, symptoms, severity ratings and notes
  • Your health picture, conditions and medications
  • Experiments, patterns and weekly reports
  • Your name and profile photo, if you add them
  • Anything read from Apple Health

None of it is uploaded to our servers. We do not have copies. We cannot retrieve it for you, and we cannot hand it to anyone else — including insurers, employers, advertisers or law enforcement — because we do not hold it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

2. Apple Health is read-only

If you grant permission, VIDA LAB reads the following from Apple Health to save you re-typing what your phone already knows:

  • Sleep analysis
  • Menstrual flow
  • Headache, abdominal cramps and fatigue
  • Step count and exercise minutes

Three commitments about this data:

  • We never write to Apple Health. The app requests read access only and holds no permission to add, change or delete anything in your Health app.
  • It is never transmitted. Health data read from Apple Health is used on-device to fill in your timeline and nothing else.
  • You can revoke it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › VIDA LAB. The app keeps working; it simply stops importing.

Anything you enter by hand always takes priority over an imported value. We never use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, or to build a profile of you, and we never share it with third parties.

3. Ask Vida answers on your phone

Ask Vida draws on a library of cited research that ships inside the app. Your questions are answered on-device and are not sent to us, to an AI provider, or to anyone else. Nothing you ask is logged off your phone.

4. What actually leaves your device

WhatWhenWho receives it
Your email address and name Only if you choose to create an account Our sign-in provider, to identify you at login
Purchase and subscription status Only if you subscribe to Vida+ Apple, and RevenueCat (our subscription processor)
A report you email yourself or your doctor Only when you tap send, to a recipient you choose Your own mail app and provider — never our servers

Accounts are optional

The entire app works without one. If you do sign in, we store only your sign-in identity — an account identifier, your email address, and your name and profile picture if your provider supplies them. We never attach health data to your account, because health data never reaches us. Your session is kept in your device's Keychain.

Reports go through your mail app

When you send a weekly report or doctor summary, VIDA LAB opens Apple's mail composer with the report attached. You choose the recipient and press send. The report travels through your own email account. It does not pass through us, and we never see it or its contents.

Subscriptions

Vida+ is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active; for that purpose RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, the product purchased, purchase and renewal dates, and basic device and country information. It receives no health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

5. What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information. Ever.
  • We do not show you ads, and we do not share data with ad networks.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use your data for advertising or data-broker purposes.
  • We do not use your health information to train machine-learning models.
  • On a Family plan, the person who pays can see the subscription. They cannot see anyone's health logs — each person's data stays on their own device.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on your device: Settings › Clear all data, inside the app. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Delete your account: Settings › Delete account. This removes your sign-in record. Since your health data was only ever on your phone, deleting the app removes the rest.
  • Revoke Apple Health access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Access, correction and portability: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal information we hold, and to object to certain processing. Because the only personal information we hold is your optional sign-in identity, these requests are usually satisfied by deleting your account — or you can email us and we will respond within 30 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

7. Retention and security

On-device data stays until you delete it or remove the app. Sign-in records are kept while your account exists and removed when you delete it. Session tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain, and connections to our sign-in provider use encrypted HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection here is architectural: the sensitive data isn't in a place we could lose.

8. Children

VIDA LAB is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Anyone under the age of majority where they live should have a parent or guardian review these terms with them before subscribing.

9. International use

We operate from the United Kingdom. If you create an account, that limited sign-in information may be processed in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, or the United States by our providers under appropriate safeguards. Your health data is not transferred anywhere, because it stays on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change is material — particularly anything that would alter what leaves your device — we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, and ask for your agreement where the law requires it.

11. Contact

Questions, requests, or anything in this document that doesn't match what you're seeing: privacy@vidalab.co.

VIDA LAB does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or claim cause and effect. It shows you patterns worth noticing, so you can have a better conversation with a clinician who can.
