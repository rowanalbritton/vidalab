# VIDA LAB — Complete Feature Brief for Ad Creation

## Brand Overview

**VIDA LAB** is a collaborative research, health-tracking, and medical education platform that translates emerging science into actionable insights for patients — especially young people navigating chronic, misunderstood, or dismissed health conditions.

**Founder:** Rowan Albritton, 18, started Vida Lab in May 2023 after years of navigating unexplained symptoms before getting an endometriosis diagnosis. She built the tool she wished she'd had.

**Mission:** Make complicated health research easier to understand. Where real stories meet real science.

**What it covers:** Cycle health, mental health, neuroscience, nutrition, fitness, and chronic illness education.

**Design aesthetic:** Quiet, intelligent femininity — cream backgrounds, forest green, elegant serif typography, generous white space, high signal / low noise.

**Important positioning notes for ads:**
- VIDA LAB is an educational platform, NOT a medical device or diagnostic tool. Always include language like "educational," "not a diagnosis," or "not a substitute for medical advice" where appropriate.
- The platform is built by a student researcher, not a licensed doctor — this is part of the brand's authenticity and relatability, especially for Gen Z / young adult audiences.
- Every feature emphasizes empowerment, self-advocacy, and turning confusing symptoms into clarity.
- All wellness content is cited and evidence-informed.

## Feature-by-Feature Descriptions

### 1. Daily Signals (Free — member account required)

**What it is:** A two-minute daily wellness check-in that tracks how you're actually feeling — and gives you back a small, relevant insight each time.

**What users track:**
- Energy level (1–5 scale)
- Sleep hours (0–14)
- Sleep quality (1–5)
- Mood (calm, happy, neutral, anxious, sad, irritable, motivated)
- Pain level (0–3)
- Cycle phase (menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal, not tracking)
- Symptoms (headache, cramps, bloating, fatigue, breast tenderness, acne, backache, nausea, brain fog, cravings, none)
- Wellness practices done today (meditation, gentle exercise, anti-inflammatory meal, supplement, breathing exercise, nature time, sleep hygiene, hydration, gratitude, stretching)
- Free-text notes

**What they get back:**
- An instant, personalized educational insight generated from their check-in (e.g., "Good energy on rested sleep is your baseline state — worth noticing so you can recognize when you drift from it.")
- A mood & energy chart showing the last 30 days
- A practice correlation view showing which wellness practices align with better days
- Full history of past check-ins (30 days free, unlimited with Vida+)
- Optional daily reminder notifications

**Ad angle:** "Two minutes a day. Real insight back. No more guessing what your body is trying to tell you."

### 2. Body Weather (Vida+ only)

**What it is:** A 7-day health forecast for your body — like a weather report, but for your energy, mood, and flare risk.

**What it shows:**
- A week-summary hero with an overall outlook statement
- 7-day forecast cards, each showing:
  - Day of week and date
  - Risk level (Low / Moderate / Elevated / High) with color coding
  - Predicted energy level (Low / Steady / High) with weather-style icons
  - Predicted mood
  - A headline for the day (e.g., "Rest before the dip")
  - Why this forecast (the patterns driving it)
  - Risk areas (specific symptoms or states to watch)
  - Recommended actions (small, proactive steps)
- "Patterns we're seeing" — recurring themes detected in your data
- "Top triggers" — what's most likely to cause flares
- "This week, focus on" — 3 numbered priority actions

**How it works:** Generated from your check-in history using pattern analysis. Requires enough data (a few weeks of check-ins) to produce a forecast.

**Ad angle:** "What if you could see a flare coming before it hit? Body Weather forecasts your week ahead — energy, mood, and flare risk — so you can act before patterns land, not after."

### 3. The Vida Differential (Vida+ only)

**What it is:** Your tracking patterns mapped to conditions worth exploring — not a diagnosis, but a starting point for anyone who feels stuck, dismissed, or unexplained.

**What it shows:**
- A summary statement of what your patterns suggest
- "Patterns we're seeing" — the specific observations detected
- Condition cards, each with:
  - Condition name
  - Match strength (Worth exploring / Moderate match / Strong pattern match)
  - Why it matches your patterns
  - Tests to consider asking for
  - Specialists to see
  - Red flags that warrant prompt evaluation
- "How to advocate for yourself" — specific advocacy notes
- "Your next steps" — numbered, actionable next steps

**Important positioning:** This is explicitly NOT a diagnosis. It's a tool to help people who've been dismissed or told "it's just anxiety" find language and direction to advocate for proper evaluation.

**Ad angle:** "Told it's 'just anxiety'? Your patterns tell a different story. The Vida Differential maps your symptoms to conditions worth exploring — with the tests to ask for, the specialists to see, and the words to advocate for yourself."

---

### 4. Vida Experiments (Vida+ only)

**What it is:** Structured n=1 self-experimentation — stop guessing if that supplement, diet, or lifestyle change actually works. Test it properly.

**What users do:**
- Create an experiment: title, intervention (what they're testing), hypothesis (what they think will happen), duration (7–90 days), metrics to watch (energy, sleep hours, sleep quality, mood, pain level, symptoms)
- Log daily adherence: did they stick to the intervention today? + optional notes
- Track progress: day number, days logged, adherence rate percentage, progress bar

**What they get:**
- Active experiments list with progress tracking
- Daily adherence log with check/circle visual indicators
- Recent log history
- On completion: AI-generated results analysis comparing their check-in data before vs. during the experiment
- Completed experiments archive

**Ad angle:** "Does magnesium actually help your sleep? Stop guessing. Vida Experiments lets you run structured self-experiments on supplements, diets, and lifestyle changes — and see, with confidence, what actually moves the needle."

### 5. Appointment Concierge / Doctor Prep (Vida+ only)

**What it is:** Walk into your doctor's appointment prepared — with a health snapshot, predicted questions, an advocacy script, and tests to ask about. So you're heard, not dismissed.

**What it includes:**
- **My Vida Health Snapshot** — a printable document showing:
  - Your name and generation date
  - Tracking period and number of check-ins
  - Key metrics: average energy, average sleep, low-energy days, poor-sleep days
  - Most frequent symptoms with counts
  - Mood summary
  - Questions to consider asking your doctor
  - Medical disclaimer
- **Treatment Log** — track current and past treatments: name, type (medication, supplement, therapy, lifestyle, procedure), dosage, start/end dates, effectiveness (1–5), side effects, status
- **Appointment Concierge** — generates a symptom narrative, advocacy script, and tests to request based on your patterns
- Print button for clean, doctor-ready documents

**Ad angle:** "Walk in prepared. Not with a vague 'I've been feeling off' — but with a health snapshot, the right questions to ask, and an advocacy script. So you're heard, not dismissed."

### 6. Local Doctor Finder (Vida+ only)

**What it is:** A searchable directory of specialists and doctors, filterable by condition category and location — no more guessing where to turn next.

**What users can do:**
- Search by name or specialty
- Filter by condition category: Autoimmune, Neurological, Cardiovascular, Endocrine, Musculoskeletal, Gastrointestinal, Respiratory, Mental Health, Chronic Pain, Dysautonomia, Gynecological, Other
- Filter by location (city, state, ZIP)
- Toggle between List view and Map view (interactive map with markers)
- See doctor details: practice name, specialty, category badge, address, phone, email, website, "accepting new patients" indicator, notes
- Book appointments directly (with optional Google Calendar sync)
- Manage upcoming appointments (view, cancel, get email reminders)

**Ad angle:** "Finding the right specialist shouldn't be a guessing game. Search our directory by condition, filter by location, and book — all in one place."

### 7. Pattern Map (Vida+ only)

**What it is:** See how your energy, mood, and symptoms correlate across your cycle and over time — the patterns worth naming to a clinician.

**What it shows:**
- Summary stats: average energy, average sleep, total check-ins
- Symptom intensity heatmap (visual calendar of symptom severity)
- Energy by cycle phase (bar chart showing average energy in menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal phases)
- "Symptoms that cluster in a phase" — top correlations (e.g., "Cramps — Luteal phase — 78%")
- Mood landscape (radial bar chart showing mood distribution)
- Printable PDF pattern report

**Important note:** Pattern Map surfaces correlations in your own data. It does not diagnose. It's designed to help you bring named patterns to a clinician.

**Ad angle:** "Your body has been telling you a story. Pattern Map helps you read it — energy, mood, and symptoms mapped across your cycle, so you can walk into your appointment with patterns, not guesswork."

### 8. Trends (Free — member account required)

**What it is:** A 90-day wide-angle view of how your energy, sleep, and mood have moved — revealing seasonal shifts and slow-moving patterns you'd miss day-to-day.

**What it shows:**
- Summary stat cards: average energy (with trend direction: up/down/steady), average sleep (with quality), most common mood, total check-ins with date range
- Energy & mood combined line chart (energy on 1–5, mood mapped to 1–5 for comparison)
- Sleep hours area chart with 8-hour target reference line
- Mood distribution bar chart (color-coded by mood)
- Most frequent symptoms (ranked bar list)

**Ad angle:** "Three months from now, you'll wish you started today. Trends shows you the long view — seasonal shifts, slow patterns, and the direction you're actually moving."

## Additional Context for Ad Creation

### Target Audience
- **Primary:** Young people (16–30) navigating chronic, misunderstood, or dismissed health conditions
- **Secondary:** Anyone — any age — dealing with unexplained symptoms who wants to track and understand their body
- **Tertiary:** People who are health-curious but not chronically ill — interested in wellness, cycle tracking, optimization

### Key Pain Points the App Addresses
1. "My doctor dismissed me / told me it's just anxiety"
2. "I can't tell if this supplement is actually doing anything"
3. "I don't know what specialist to see for my symptoms"
4. "I forget what I was feeling last month and can't see patterns"
5. "Medical research is too dense — I can't understand what's relevant to me"
6. "I'm tired of guessing what my body needs"
7. "I want to advocate for myself but don't have the words"
8. "I want to eat/live better for my condition but don't know where to start"

### Brand Voice
- Warm, intelligent, not clinical
- Empowering, not fear-mongering
- Honest about uncertainty
- "In your corner" — personal, not corporate
- Young but not immature — respects the reader's intelligence
- Accessible without being simplistic

### Design / Visual Notes for Ads
- Color palette: Cream (#F9F8F5), Forest green (#2E463E), Moss (#3c6b4f), Sky blue (#BDE0E9), Taupe (#756a59)
- Typography: Elegant serifs (Newsreader) for headings, clean sans-serif (DM Sans) for body
- Aesthetic: Quiet, intelligent, spacious, editorial — think a wellness brand that reads like a research journal
- Imagery: Natural light, soft textures, real people, not stock-photo-cheesy

### Mandatory Disclaimers for Ads
- VIDA LAB is an educational platform, not a medical device
- Features do not diagnose conditions
- Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions
- Founded by a student researcher, not a licensed doctor
