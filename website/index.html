THE VIDA LAB APP

Your health story,
made easier to see.

Built for people living with symptoms that are difficult to summarize. Log how you feel in under a minute, see correlations worth noticing, prepare for appointments, and keep the underlying health data private.

Private by design. Health entries are encrypted on-device before optional backup. VIDA LAB is a wellness reflection tool, not a diagnosis tool or medical record.

BUILT FROM CURIOSITY

A lab for understanding what comes next.

VIDA LAB was created to make emerging health research more understandable, especially for teens and young women navigating chronic illness, pain, confusing symptoms and a medical system that can be hard to navigate.

It sits at the intersection of health education, biomedical innovation and personal pattern awareness: rigorous enough to respect the science, human enough to be useful.
